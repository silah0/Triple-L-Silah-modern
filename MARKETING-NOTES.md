# Triple L — Portfolio Marketing Notes

## What this showcase demonstrates

- A branded mobile-first digital menu.
- Browsable categories and instant product search.
- Product details without online ordering or checkout.
- A cohesive visual identity and a responsive customer experience.
- A concept that can be adapted to a real café's brand, menu, and location.

## Suggested presentation angle

Present Triple L as a **sample digital-menu concept created by SILAH**, not as an operating café. The message should focus on the experience a café owner can have for their own business: a branded menu, easy browsing, and a QR-ready public link.

## Before publishing promotional material

- Use screenshots from the final published website, not development screenshots.
- Include the public demo link once hosting is active.
- Generate the QR code only after the final public URL is confirmed.
- Do not describe the demo prices, address, or opening hours as real business information.
- Use the included `assets/images/triple-l-social-preview.jpg` as a link-preview asset; update the absolute image URL in the page metadata after hosting.
